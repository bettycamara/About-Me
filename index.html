About Me

My name is Betty Camara, and I am a journalism student at the CUNY Graduate School of Journalism at Craig Newmark.
I have a strong passion for photojournalism and hope to pursue it as my future career. I am also very interested in data journalism.
However, although I am eager to learn more about it, I sometimes find data analysis challenging to understand.
I hope to strengthen my skills in this area and become more confident in using data to tell compelling stories.

My Top Three Favorite Stories

    The article Photo Editors Who Made a Difference is about how photo editors help photographers improve their work and build successful careers.

  2. Magic Moments
    The article talks about Cartier-Bresson one, who I believe is the father of modern day Photojournalism.
